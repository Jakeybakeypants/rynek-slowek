      Rynek Słówek

      Walk around a Polish market square, talk to the people there and run their errands. Every Polish word is coloured by how well you know it.

      • jabłkoPurple: new. You haven't met it or sorted it yet.
      • kawaOrange: you're learning it.
      • dzieńGreen: you know it.

      Click any word, in a speech bubble or floating over a thing, to see what it means and sort it.
